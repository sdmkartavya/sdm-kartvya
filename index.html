FAQ

Allow location access in your browser/site settings, then reload.
Camera doesn't show?

Forgot to check out?
Contact your admin - they can review and correct it from the Admin panel.
Face not matching?
Retry with good lighting, no cap/mask, holding the phone the way you did at registration.
